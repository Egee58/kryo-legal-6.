## 2. About this application

Kryo Video Uploader is a privately operated tool used to create, process and prepare short-form videos for the operator's own TikTok accounts.

The application supports German and English video production. It is not intended as a public service for general users.

## 3. Data that may be processed

Depending on the functions used, the application may process:

- TikTok account information, such as account identifiers and usernames.
- Authentication information, including access tokens and refresh tokens required for TikTok authorization.
- Video files, scripts, captions and generated audio.
- Technical information and error logs required to operate and troubleshoot the application.

## 4. Third-party services

The application uses the following third-party services:

**TikTok:** Used for account authorization and video uploads. TikTok may process account information and uploaded content according to its own privacy policy.

**ElevenLabs:** Used to generate spoken audio from text. Text submitted for speech generation may be processed by ElevenLabs.

**Anthropic Claude:** Used to assist with script and content generation. Text and prompts submitted to the service may be processed by Anthropic.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Their processing may involve servers located outside the European Economic Area.

## 5. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed only to operate the application, generate videos, authorize TikTok accounts, upload videos and maintain technical functionality.

Where applicable, processing is based on the controller's legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR or another applicable legal basis.

## 6. Storage and retention

Video files, scripts, generated audio, configuration data and technical records are stored locally on the operator's Windows computer.

TikTok authentication tokens and other credentials required to operate the application are also stored locally on that computer.

The application does not intentionally use a separate cloud storage service for these local files.

Data is retained locally until it is no longer needed and is deleted by the operator. Third-party providers may retain data submitted to their services according to their own terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Data sharing

Personal data is not sold. Data may be transmitted to the third-party services listed in Section 4 where necessary for the application's functions.

The application is not intended to share data with unrelated third parties.

## 8. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect locally stored data and authentication information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.
